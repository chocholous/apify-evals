## Test
Explain what a closure is in programming. Answer in English, 2-3 sentences.

## Checkpoint
The explanation must mention that a closure captures/retains/remembers variables from its enclosing scope (or lexical environment). Must not incorrectly state that closures are the same as anonymous functions or lambdas.

---

## Test
Is a tomato a fruit or a vegetable? Give a short answer in English.

## Checkpoint
The answer must acknowledge that a tomato is botanically a fruit but culinarily treated as a vegetable. An answer that says only "fruit" or only "vegetable" without nuance should fail.
