## Test
Explain in 2-3 sentences why the sky appears blue. Answer in English.

## Checkpoint
The answer must mention Rayleigh scattering or the scattering of light by the atmosphere, and must be scientifically accurate.

## Test
Name three programming languages that were created in the 1990s. Answer in English.

## Checkpoint
The answer must list exactly three programming languages, all of which were actually created in the 1990s (e.g., Java, Python, Ruby, PHP, JavaScript, Haskell are acceptable).
